About

Built from scratch.

Delaware, Ohio → 750K followers → building AI systems. Here's how it happened.

Where It Started

I started flipping Instagram theme pages and trading accounts at 14. Not because someone told me to — because I was bored in a small town and the internet had rules I could figure out. By the time I graduated, I'd already learned more about audience growth than most marketing classes teach.

Five Years in the Trenches

In 2021 I joined CardCollector2 as a one-person content operation. Over five years I built and ran the full social media engine — strategy, production, scheduling, analytics, and systems — for a sports media channel that grew to 750K+ combined followers. I did it without a team and without a playbook.

Building on the Side

While running CC2, I co-founded How It's Filmed with Reese White. We grew it to 175K Instagram followers, built 100+ production tools, and built a fully gated app. Every piece of it was built in evenings and weekends. None of it was handed to us.

What I'm Building Now

I left CC2 in May 2026 to go fully independent. I'm taking on Columbus retainer clients for content strategy and custom digital tools, building AI-powered workflows, and developing creator revenue systems. The same scrappy approach — just pointed at my own thing.
