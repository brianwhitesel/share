Launch Approach — 6 Phases, ~3 Weeks
The Model (Layla Greenhill / Iman framework)
Validate before building. Move Dylan's audience from Instagram → WhatsApp waitlist → Whop community. No product fully built before demand is confirmed. 3-day enrollment window with founding member price lock as the urgency mechanism. Dylan posts — I script, build, and execute. He has nothing to lose.
  • Phase 1Instagram Stories build-up — test demand, tease something's coming, screenshot DM flood
  • Phase 2Move interested followers into a private WhatsApp waitlist group via story link

  • Phase 4Full offer breakdown day before launch — $9/mo founding rate revealed, open time set
  • Phase 5Enrollment opens — drop link, poll the group on objections, message every person who voted
  • Phase 6Urgency close — 12 hrs, 3 hrs, 1 hr countdown. Follow through on every deadline
