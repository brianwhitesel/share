Amend the Operating Agreement

Action needed — adds you as co-member

The operating agreement is the internal document that defines who owns what and how decisions get made. Right now it only has my name. We need to amend it to formally make this a 50/50 partnership with both names on it.

This is the most important step — everything else is downstream of this.

What it covers

  • 50/50 ownership split between Brian Whitesel and Reese [last name]
  • Decision-making structure (how major decisions get made)
  • Revenue distribution (how profits get divided)
  • What happens if one person wants to exit

How to do it

  • Option A: Use a service like Northwest Registered Agent (~$100–150, they draft it)
  • Option B: Local business attorney (more expensive, but reviewed by a lawyer)
  • We can also draft a simple amendment ourselves and have it notarized — cheapest option
3

Get an EIN

Action needed — free, takes 10 minutes

An EIN (Employer Identification Number) is basically a Social Security number for the business. We need it to open a business bank account and to properly separate business income from personal income.

How to get it

  • Go to irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/apply-for-an-employer-identification-number-ein-online
  • Takes about 10 minutes online
  • It's free — don't use a paid service for this
  • You'll get the number instantly

Note: One of us does this (either works). The EIN is tied to the business entity, not the individual applying.
